*Published: September 21, 2026*

Google has introduced the Googlebook, a new line of premium laptops starting at $899, designed to deliver high performance and durability. The lineup features five distinct models crafted from premium materials, equipped with advanced processors for efficient AI tasks, and includes a 12-month subscription to Google AI Pro along with other software perks. Pre-orders are currently available, with retail availability set for early October.
