Google has announced the launch of the Googlebook, a new line of premium laptops designed to seamlessly integrate with Android phones, now available for pre-order starting at $899. These laptops feature advanced AI tools, high-end hardware, and a secure architecture, allowing users to easily transition between their phone and laptop while accessing files and apps. Pre-orders can be made through the Google Store and select retailers, with devices set to hit the shelves on October 4, 2026.
