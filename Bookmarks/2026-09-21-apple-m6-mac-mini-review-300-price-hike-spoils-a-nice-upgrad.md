# [Apple M6 Mac mini review: $300 price hike spoils a nice upgrade - Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/09/apple-m6-mac-mini-review-300-price-hike-spoils-a-nice-upgrade/)

*Published: September 21, 2026*

The review of the Apple M6 Mac mini highlights that while the new model offers solid upgrades in performance with its advanced chip architecture, the $300 price increase to $899 for the base model diminishes its value compared to the previous M4 version. The design remains largely unchanged, with improvements in CPU and GPU cores, but the price hike makes it less appealing as a general-purpose desktop option. Overall, the M6 Mac mini is seen as a capable machine, but its affordability is a significant drawback.
