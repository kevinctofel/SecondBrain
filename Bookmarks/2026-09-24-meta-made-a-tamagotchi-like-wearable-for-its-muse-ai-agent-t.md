*Published: September 24, 2026*

Meta has introduced a Tamagotchi-like wearable device called Muse Charm, designed to keep users connected to its personal AI agent, Muse. Revealed by CEO Mark Zuckerberg during the company's annual Connect event, the device features a small screen displaying a digital avatar and allows users to interact with Muse through voice commands. Although not yet available for purchase, it is expected to ship in time for the holiday season, offering a playful and accessible way for consumers to engage with AI technology.
