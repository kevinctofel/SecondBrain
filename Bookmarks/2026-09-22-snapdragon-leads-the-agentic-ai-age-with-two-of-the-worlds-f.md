*Published: September 22, 2026*

Qualcomm has announced the launch of two new flagship mobile platforms, the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 and Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6, designed to enhance mobile experiences in the agentic AI age. These platforms offer advanced AI capabilities, improved gaming performance, and enhanced camera features, catering to the needs of consumers and OEM partners alike. Devices powered by these new SoCs will be featured in flagship smartphones from various leading brands, marking a significant step in mobile technology innovation.
