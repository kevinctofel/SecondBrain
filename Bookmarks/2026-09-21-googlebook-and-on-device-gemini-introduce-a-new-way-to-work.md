*Published: September 21, 2026*

Googlebook, equipped with on-device Gemini, transforms the laptop experience by integrating advanced AI features like Magic Pointer, Rambler voice dictation, and customizable widgets. These tools streamline tasks such as scheduling, organizing voice notes, and creating personalized widgets without coding. Priced starting at $899, Googlebook is available for pre-order, with shipments beginning October 4, 2026, and includes a 12-month subscription to AI tools and cloud storage.
