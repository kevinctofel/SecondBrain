*Published: September 21, 2026*

The Acer Googlebook 14 has been officially launched as the only 360° convertible device in Google's new hardware lineup, starting at $899. It features a sleek design with a 14-inch 3K OLED multitouch display, a versatile 4-mode hinge, and is powered by Intel's latest processors, offering strong performance and battery life. The device emphasizes sustainability, constructed with 49% circular materials and comes with a range of bundled software and services.
